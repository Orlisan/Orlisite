**Apocalyptic Scythes** adds 5 powerful scythes to Minecraft, each with a unique ability activated by right-clicking.

***

### Scythes

**Fire Scythe** — Summons fire structures and explosions in front of you. Grants fire resistance and slow falling after use.

**Ice Scythe** — Creates ice structures that freeze nearby enemies. Surrounds them with powder snow for an extra effect.

**Soul Scythe** — Has two modes: right-click to place soul sand and soul fire traps, shift+right-click to absorb souls from nearby entities, temporarily increasing your attack damage.

**Time Scythe** — Creates a time bubble that slows all entities around you whilst granting you a speed boost. The bubble is visible as a temporary structure.

**Black Hole Scythe** — Creates a black hole that pulls all nearby entities towards a single point.

***

### Upgrades

All scythes can be upgraded using **Scythe Essence**, combinable at an anvil to increase damage. The level cost increases with each upgrade.

A scythe can be transformed into another using the Transformer block and Scythe Essence. Useful for duplicates!

***

### Loot

Scythe Essence and scythes can be found in the loot chests of specific structures, including Ancient Cities, Bastions, End Cities, Igloos, Pillager Outposts, Ruined Portals, Taiga Villages, and Woodland Mansions.
