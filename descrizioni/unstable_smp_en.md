# Unstable SMP Death Sounds - Optimized
**An improvement of the Unstable SMP Death Sounds mod by BattryLeaf.**
This mod adds the Wither spawn sound when a player dies, just like in the original mod, but fixes compatibility issues with non-English language servers, removes unnecessary mixins for better performance, and increases reliability across different server configurations.
**Based on: [Unstable SMP Death Sounds di BattryLeaf](https://modrinth.com/mod/unstable-smp-death-sounds-by-bl)**
